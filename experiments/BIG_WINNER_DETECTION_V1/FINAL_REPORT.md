# BIG_WINNER_DETECTION_V1 — final bounded report

## Answer to the original question

**INCONCLUSIVE historically, for every mechanism.**

This does not mean that no mechanism detected large winners. It means the available
evidence cannot support the requested strict point-in-time test without violating
its own stop conditions. No precision, recall, lift, early-hit count, winner card or
“best factor” is reported because no real signal freeze or valid total-return label
panel exists in this V1 execution.

- isolated worktree and dedicated experiment branch;
- source-state snapshot and audit of repository, mechanisms and external databases;
- pre-outcome protocol, system contract, detector registry and replay classification;
- separate signal, freeze, outcome and evaluation modules;
- deterministic selection/scorability contracts;
- hash-verified one-time freeze implementation;
- next-open/12-calendar-month outcome mechanics;
- episode, precocity, matched-random, negative-control, block-bootstrap and Holm code;
- positive leakage control and contract tests.

## Why the real experiment stopped

The absolute PIT contract requires demonstrable source availability at each signal.
The recovered historical databases do not provide complete publication/availability,
revision, ticker mapping and action lineage for all required inputs. The outcome side
also lacks universe-complete cash/stock events and valid terminal economics. The
project catalog explicitly marks economic completeness false; the latest audit still
records material unresolved events and intervals.

Generating labels from adjusted or last-observed prices would answer a weaker,
different question and could introduce survivorship, corporate-action and
availability bias. The protocol explicitly forbids that substitution.

Provide an immutable, versioned panel that proves decision-time availability and
issuer/ticker identity, plus complete total-return events and terminal settlements
